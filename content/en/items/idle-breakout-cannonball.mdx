## What Makes the Cannonball Unique in Idle Breakout?

Most projectiles in the game bounce off obstacles after dealing damage. The cannonball introduces a unique passive mechanic known as "cleaving." Whenever a cannonball strikes a brick whose current hit points are lower than the ball's raw attack power, the projectile does not ricochet. Instead, it tears directly through the brick, obliterating it instantly while maintaining its trajectory and velocity.

Because of this piercing behavior, a properly upgraded cannonball can slice across an entire grid in a single pass. When brick clusters pack tightly together, ordinary balls lose momentum bouncing between walls. A high-damage cannonball turns that cluster into instant gold.

Community reports and player experiences consistently rank this projectile alongside the Sniper Ball as one of the two best primary damage dealers in the game. Even right out of the gate with no speed or attack upgrades, a single purchased unit outputs roughly 250 damage per second (DPS) purely through its raw base stats.

However, expanding your fleet requires careful financial planning. Unlike several other units that scale their purchase price at a milder rate, the cannonball uses an aggressive price scaling model:

* **Base Cost:** $75,000

Because of this 30% exponential increase, your **idle breakout cannonball** fleet can quickly become more expensive than balls unlocked later in the game. ## Upgrade Paths: Maximizing Power and Speed

Raw numbers govern how smoothly your cannonballs glide across the play area. Because the piercing mechanic requires your ball's power to strictly exceed a brick's health, falling behind on power upgrades causes the ball to bounce instead of cleaving, reducing its clears per minute.

## Skill Tree Synergies: Tree 4 Upgrades

In the skill tree menu, row 4 directly alters how your cannonballs interact with the board. Unlocking these nodes requires spending precious Skill Points (SP) gained through prestige runs. 

Here is how the skill path unfolds:

| Node ID | Skill Name / Effect | Cost (SP) | Cumulative SP | Prerequisites |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4,0** | Cannonballs Always Damage Black Bricks | 1 | 1 | None |
| **4,1** | Lasers Always Damage Black Bricks | 2 | 3 | Node 3,0 |
| **4,2** | Cannonballs Have a Small Chance to Damage All Bricks at Once | 4 | 7 | Nodes 4,0 & 4,1 |
| **4,3** | Enable Mouse Hold to Click | 8 | 15 | Nodes 4,0, 4,1, & 4,2 |

### Breaking Through Black Bricks (Node 4,0)

Black bricks present a major obstacle for automated idle builds because they normally resist standard collisions or require high click counts. Spending a single skill point on Node 4,0 allows your **idle breakout cannonball** to strike black bricks without penalty. This single point completely eliminates mid-round progression stalls.

### Screen-Wide Cleaves (Node 4,2)

Node 4,2 introduces a percentage roll on every collision. When this ability triggers, the cannonball deals its full attack value to every single brick visible on screen simultaneously. In later stages where hundreds of weakened bricks populate the grid, a single lucky proc can clear the entire board instantly, granting massive gold spikes.

### Active Utility (Node 4,3)

While the cannonball is an automated projectile, completing Tree 4 unlocks Node 4,3: *Enable Mouse Hold to Click*. For players who prefer a hybrid active-idle playstyle, this perk lets you hold down the left mouse button to auto-strike bricks, drastically speeding up clears alongside your high-velocity cannonballs.

## Prestige Strategy and Deployment Timing

Knowing when to buy your first **idle breakout cannonball** dictates how quickly you progress through prestige cycles. 

### First Run vs. Second and Third Prestige

On your very first playthrough, saving up $75,000 requires patience. Most players spend their early capital on Basic, Plasma, and Scatter Balls. While you should unlock at least one cannonball during your initial run to begin accumulating damage toward the 1 Million Total Damage achievement, do not overspend on quantity early.

Player experience shows that cannonballs truly shine after your second or third prestige reset. By that point, your global prestige power multipliers make the $75,000 threshold trivial within minutes of starting a new run. 

### Recommended Mid-Run Ball Ratio

To keep your board clear speed balanced, avoid spending 100% of your budget on a single projectile type. Use the following deployment ratio as a general template:

| Projectile Type | Recommended Quantity Ratio | Core Operational Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Ball** | 10–15 | Cheap triggers for click buffs |
| **Plasma Ball** | 5–10 | Splash damage against tight clusters |
| **Sniper Ball** | 10–15 | Single-target boss and elite brick elimination |
| **Cannonball** | 8–12 | High-speed lane clearing and wave piercing |
| **Poison Ball** | 5–8 | Persistent damage-over-time debuffing |

Pairing the Sniper Ball with the cannonball creates a devastating loop: snipers eliminate high-HP anchor bricks, lowering average board health and letting your cannonballs cleave freely through the remaining blocks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my idle breakout cannonball bouncing instead of piercing through bricks?
Your cannonball bounces whenever the remaining health of the target brick exceeds the ball's current attack power. To restore the piercing cleave effect, upgrade your cannonball's power stat until its damage exceeds the current wave's brick health pool.

### How do I raise the cannonball's speed limit beyond 40?
To bypass the default speed cap of 40 and hit the hard ceiling of 60, you must purchase the 10x "Ball Speed Increase" upgrade in the Prestige shop and unlock the "Increase Speed Limit to 60" skill node located in the Sniper Ball's skill tree.

### Should I prioritize buying more cannonballs or upgrading my existing ones?
In the early-to-mid stages of a run, upgrading power is almost always better than buying additional balls. Because the cost of new cannonballs escalates by 30% per purchase, adding extra balls with weak attack values will only clutter your screen with bouncing projectiles rather than clean, piercing strikes. Upgrade power first, push speed to 20+, and only then buy additional units.
