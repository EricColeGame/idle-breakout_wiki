While some achievements occur naturally through idle runtime, others demand targeted strategies. For instance, relying exclusively on auto-progression will delay specific damage thresholds, particularly when dealing with specialized damage types like Plasma or Basic ball outputs.

### 1. Basic Ball: 1 Million Total Damage
The first real milestone you encounter tests your early game pacing. Basic balls have low base damage and moderate speed, meaning you cannot rely on sheer brute force. Reaching one million damage requires steady investment in base ball power and speed upgrades before transitioning into high-cost variants.

### 2. Plasma Ball: 1 Million Total Damage
Plasma balls deal splash damage, making them exceptional crowd-clearers against clustered bricks. Because their damage spreads across adjacent targets, you can reach this one million threshold quickly once you invest in their explosion radius and base damage upgrades during your second or third run.

### 3. Have 10 of Each Ball
This is one of the steepest economic walls among all **idle breakout achievements**. Because ball purchase costs scale exponentially with every purchase of that class, acquiring 10 of the high-tier balls (such as Poison and Sniper) requires massive gold multipliers fueled by late-game prestige cycles.

### 4. Break 1,000 Black Bricks
Black Bricks spawn deeper into level progressions and yield Black Brick currency upon destruction. Because they feature high health pools and appear at specific tier intervals, cracking 1,000 of them demands consistent runs deep into the triple-digit levels.

### 5. Reach Level 250
Level 250 serves as the gateway to the true endgame. Bricks at this level possess millions of hit points, rendering raw clicking useless. Passing this milestone confirms that your automated DPS, ball velocity, and prestige multiplier loop are working correctly.

### 6. Unlock All 4 Lasers
The perimeter lasers (Top, Bottom, Left, Right) provide continuous passive beam damage to any brick crossing their line of sight. Unlocking all four requires dedicated gold allocation early in a run, bypassing ball purchases temporarily to establish a permanent screen-clearing failsafe.

### 7. Break 1 Million Bricks (Grand Prize)
The ultimate attrition achievement in the game. Breaking one million total bricks requires millions of ball bounces and sustained idle runtime. Completing this grants community-celebrated milestone status and cements your progression mastery.

### Mastering the 1M Ball Damage Benchmarks
To unlock the damage achievements for Basic and Plasma balls, avoid spreading your early gold across all ball types immediately:

1. **For Basic Balls:** Keep your active ball roster small during your opening run. Upgrade Basic Ball Power to at least Level 20 and Speed to Level 15 before buying more advanced ball classes. This forces the Basic Ball to land the finishing blows on higher-health bricks, rapidly logging damage into your tracker.
2. **For Plasma Balls:** Once unlocked, Plasma balls benefit heavily from crowded boards. Do not upgrade ball speed too quickly; keep them at moderate speed so they collide repeatedly with dense brick walls, triggering maximum splash damage procs.

### Conquering the "10 of Each Ball" Economic Hurdle
Attempting this challenge on a fresh, non-prestiged save is virtually impossible due to cost scaling. Player experience shows that attempting to hoard gold without prestige multipliers stalls your progress around ball number seven or eight in the advanced tiers.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Idle Breakout Achievements

### Do idle breakout achievements reset when you prestige?
No. Your achievement progress and completed milestones are permanent across all prestige cycles. Resetting your run via prestige only clears your current level, active balls, and current gold balance, allowing you to build back faster with prestige bonuses intact.

### What is the fastest way to get the 1 Million Plasma Ball damage achievement?
The fastest method is to rush unlocking the Plasma Ball, upgrade its splash radius, and hold off on buying Scatter or Cannon balls for a short window. This ensures Plasma explosions claim the bulk of total damage dealt across dense brick clusters between levels 50 and 150.

### Why is the "Have 10 of Each Ball" achievement so difficult?
Ball costs scale exponentially with each purchase within a tier. The final tiers—specifically Poison and Sniper balls—reach billions in gold costs for their eighth, ninth, and tenth purchases. Completing this milestone requires high prestige gold multipliers to generate sufficient cash flow.

### Can you complete all idle breakout achievements without manual clicking?
Yes. Aside from the opening moments of a fresh run where a few manual clicks help buy your initial Basic Ball, every achievement in *Idle Breakout* can be earned through automated ball bounces, laser beams, and passive idle systems.
