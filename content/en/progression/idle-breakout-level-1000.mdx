### The Poison and Scatter Synergy

The absolute backbone of any deep-stage push is the interplay between the Poison Ball and the Scatter Ball. Poison applies a stacking damage-over-time debuff that softens durable bricks. Because poison damage scales proportionally, it strips through billions of effective health points where flat-damage balls stall out.

Once bricks are infected, Scatter Balls enter the fray. Upon impact, Scatter Balls fragment into sub-projectiles that ricochet across the entire screen. Community reports confirm that a board saturated with poison stacks melts almost instantly when peppered by dozens of miniature scatter pellets.

### The Role of Plasma and Sniper Balls

While Poison and Scatter do the heavy lifting, Plasma Balls provide crucial localized splash damage to crack tightly clustered brick formations. A single well-upgraded Sniper Ball serves as an elite assassin, automatically targeting the highest-health brick on screen to prevent stubborn obstacles from stalling your automated stage transitions.
