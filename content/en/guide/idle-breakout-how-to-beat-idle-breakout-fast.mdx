## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single best ball in Idle Breakout?
While early players lean heavily on the Plasma ball for its visual area damage, the **Cannon Ball** paired with the **Sniper Ball** constitutes the most effective late-game combination. Cannon balls provide the raw damage necessary to shatter high-tier bricks, while Sniper balls automatically lock onto the toughest targets to guarantee zero wasted bounces.

### In Idle Breakout how to beat Idle Breakout fast if progress stalls around level 300?
If your progress slows to a crawl around level 300, you have waited too long to prestige. Reset your run immediately to convert your cleared stages into Black Bricks. Spend those points on global ball speed and starting damage multipliers, then rebuild your ball slots using primarily Snipers, Scatters, and Cannons rather than basic balls.

### Does clicking remain useful in late-game stages?
Clicking damage drops off severely after level 100 compared to autonomous ball scaling. In late-game stages, manual clicking should be reserved almost exclusively for popping high-value bonus bricks (such as Gold or Black bricks) rather than attempting to damage standard health blocks manually.

### How do Poison balls interact with high-health bricks?
Poison balls apply a damage-over-time debuff that reduces brick health by a percentage rather than a flat damage number. This makes them exceptionally powerful against late-game stages and fortified blocks, especially when Cannon balls strike immediately afterward to finish off the weakened bricks.
